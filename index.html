Meridian — what this build shows

One beach, a software raycaster, and three peer checks. npm start only serves these files and introduces browsers. It does not decide a hit, a dig, or a death.

Pass means the sentence under each check actually happens. A red error, two different hashes, or a page that stays on “waiting” is a fail.

1. Walk

You stand on the south beach, looking north. The picture is 160×90, scaled up. The ground is the integer crust. The sky is blue because the sun is up.

Click the picture, then look with the mouse. W A S D move. A and D strafe; they do not turn you. Shift sprints. Space jumps and lands. Walk north and the wooden compound gets closer.

Press 3 for the bit. Look down and hold the left mouse for about a second. Pass: a darker pit opens in the sand, and the line reads voxel 500 plus a hash.

Press 1 for the line. The pad view is already aimed at the dummy. Click it. Pass: chest 28 and dummy 72. Four hits and it reads dummy dead, then it stands back up after 5 seconds.

Open the walk

2. Position

Two browsers trade an unreliable position. No island is drawn.

Pass: both logs say waiting for the other tab or waiting for the offer, then offered or answered, then snap open and remote x,y,z lines that change. Open either tab first. If a log says lighthouse failed, npm start is not running.
