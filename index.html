Meridian — what this build shows

Newest check first. npm start only serves these files and introduces browsers. It does not decide a hit, a dig, or a death.

Pass means the sentence under the check actually happens. A red error, two different results, or a page that stays on “waiting” is a fail.

1. Reflections

The sand reflection is smooth. The bands are gone. Look up and to the right: the sun is the bright disc, and it is the light. Lit sand is warm. A wall or the dummy throws a shadow. The sun also shows in the sand, as one glint, not stripes.

Pass: look down and the sand is cooler than a flat brown, with the sky in it. From the pad, the dummy shows the beach and the sky on its curve, not a flat red circle.

2. Pace

Open the walk and leave this window focused. The corner reads a frame rate, and the line ends in vsync and a pixel size. The picture follows the screen.

Click another window so the walk is still visible but not focused. Pass: the corner falls to about 1 fps and the line says 1fps. Click the walk again and the corner climbs back with the screen.

If a frame takes longer than the screen, the pixel size steps down until the picture keeps up. It steps down further if that is what it takes to stay near 30 fps, and steps back up when the frames are short. The floor is a quarter of the window.
