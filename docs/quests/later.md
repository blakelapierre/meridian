# Not yet

These are the quest shapes the concept allows. None of them are in the client.

## A fellowship offer

Two people in sense range who have both accepted. While it lasts, map knowledge is offered at friend tier. It ends when someone leaves. No giver. No reward but the sight.

## A road

Two strangers cut a path. If someone pays the upkeep, it is there next month. The quest is the road still existing, not a marker on it.

## A patron

An allegiance offer: land, rescue, or a hearth full of people, in exchange for a tithe that was named out loud. Refusing is allowed. A patron who stops providing can be left.

## A strike that is not the atoll

A core, a site, a buy, a plant. You walked there. You were not queued. Winning pays the company in ground or materials. Walking out pays nothing.

## Refused shapes

Kill counts. Collect ten. Escort. A map pin from a stranger you have not met. A daily. A battle pass. A chapter that completes when you enter a volume and listen.
