# The atoll paper

The crate holds a paper. Its four lines are lore, in [atoll](../lore/atoll.md): the compound is north, the bit digs, a hearth binds, the fin drinks sun.

The build does not show those lines. Nothing on the screen asks you to complete them, in order or otherwise. There is no completion state.

If your skills are high enough, the notebook under the frame rate may later record a fact in your own words:

| Skill | At least | What you might write |
|---|---|---|
| Mining | 4 | The sand took a hole |
| Mining | 8 | You put the sand back |
| Building | 1 | You raised a wall |
| Building | 4 | A hearth stands, or you bound to it |
| Building | 8 | The fin is set |
| Appraisal | 8 | The sentence you got from identifying something |
| Appraisal | 20 | The compound runs a strike |

Below the number, that sentence is not written. Doing the thing is not itself a note.

A stranger is told nothing. An acquaintance can hear one recent sentence, if you can phrase it. A friend can hear up to three, if appraisal or mapping is high enough. The listener keeps a sentence only when their own appraisal or mapping can read it. The line says `heard a note`. It does not list a goal.
