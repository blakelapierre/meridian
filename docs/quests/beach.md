# The atoll paper

Four errands. They are the crate paper from [atoll lore](../lore/atoll.md). The build shows the first unfinished one.

| Order | Line | Completes when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | dig the beach | The bit writes a hole |
| 2 | bind a hearth | E on a hearth you crafted |
| 3 | set the fin | The hammer finishes the fin |
| 4 | enter the compound | You join the strike |

You may do them out of order. The line still walks the list from the top, so a bound hearth does not speak until the sand has been dug. That is the paper's order, not a gate. Digging is not locked behind the hearth.

There is no turn-in. The dummy is not a quest mob. Dying and recalling does not advance the paper. Leaving the compound is the strike's own rule, not a fifth errand.

When the fourth is done, the line disappears. It does not offer a sequel.
