# MERIDIAN
## Keymap

Slice keys. Later screens do not get to steal these. Hands are first person: the mouse looks, the body does not turn with A or D.

| Key | Hands | With the bit | With the hammer | Strike buy window |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mouse move | Look | Look | Look | Look |
| W A S D | Move. A strafe left, D strafe right | same | same | same |
| Shift (hold) | Sprint | Sprint | Sprint | Sprint |
| Ctrl (hold) | Crouch | Crouch | Crouch | Crouch |
| Space | Jump | Jump | Jump | Jump |
| Mouse 1 (hold) | Fire or melee | Channel the brush | Place the selected piece | — |
| Mouse 2 | — | — | Salvage the piece under the cursor | — |
| R | Reload | — | Rotate yaw 90° | — |
| C | — | Cycle dig, fill, smooth | — | — |
| E | Use, enter a strike prompt | Use | Use | Use |
| F | Swap to previous weapon | — | — | — |
| V | Mallet, even if another weapon is out | — | — | — |
| 1 | Primary | — | — | — |
| 2 | Secondary | — | — | — |
| 3 | Tool (bit or hammer) | — | — | — |
| 4 | Select utility. Press again to cycle the four strike slots | — | — | — |
| Wheel | Follow distance and height. Scroll out to sit farther and higher | same | same | same |
| G | Throw the selected utility | — | — | — |
| B | — | — | — | Buy menu. Hidden outside the window |
| Tab | Cycle people. Shift goes backward | same | same | same |
| M | Sensed map. Coordinates and heading always. Range, people, names, and objects grow with mapping | same | same | same |
| Esc | Close the open panel and clear the action target | same | same | same |
| Q | Toggle auto run. S still backs up | same | same | same |
| Backquote | Cycle objects. Shift goes backward. Not people, not sand | same | same | same |

Use is one line in the world: the crate, the strike prompt, the hearth. There is no second interact key.

Reserved and **unused** in the slice: the war table. When it exists, the seated keys replace movement with pan. W A S D pan. A pans left. The wheel zooms. That camera is not a vehicle.

If a chase-cam vehicle is ever added, A turns it left and D turns it right while it moves forward. Do not copy the FPS strafe signs into that camera.

## Touch

On a phone or tablet (a coarse pointer), the walk shows touch controls. `?touch=1` forces them on and `?touch=0` turns them off. A desktop with a mouse never sees them, even if its screen takes touch. Pointer lock is not used on touch: the look pad turns the camera directly.

| Touch | Same as |
|---|---|
| Left stick | W and S forward and back, Z and C strafe (the movement keys the walk reads). Push fully forward to sprint (Shift) |
| Drag on the right side | Mouse move: look |
| Tap on the right side | Mouse 1 click: fire, or channel the bit or place a piece for one tap |
| Fire (hold) | Mouse 1 held |
| Jump (hold) | Space |
| Crouch (hold) | Ctrl |
| E Use | E |
| R | R |
| 1 Line | 1 |
| 3 Tool | 3, bit and hammer. Hold Fire with the bit out to dig |
| G Throw | G |
| B Buy | B |
| Q Auto | Q |
| Tab | Tab |
| M Map | M |
| Esc | Esc: close the panel and clear the target |
| Info | Show or hide the help, skills and network panels, hidden by default on touch |

Not on touch yet: Mouse 2 (salvage and orbit), the wheel (follow distance), C to cycle dig modes, F, V, 2, 4, Backquote and the hammer keys.
