# Atoll

The first island. A beach, a ruined footing, a practice dummy, and a compound with a core. It is a place on Cairn, not a tutorial map that deletes itself.

## What is here

The footing was already in the sand when you woke. Nobody in the build claims to have poured it. The crate beside the yard holds a bit, metal, stone, a fin, and a paper. The paper's four lines are the compound is north, the bit digs, a hearth binds, the fin drinks sun. The client does not display that paper and does not track it.

The dummy is padded linen on a stake. It is not a person and it does not vote. Standing on it is how you learn that the wilderness hits back. The compound is a strike volume. Inside, you are a round. Outside, you are your body again.

## What happened, as far as the beach knows

Someone ran a contract here often enough to leave a footing and a core. They are not in the scene. The sea is the edge of this cell, not the edge of the world. North of the doors is the strike. South of the dummy is open sand.

## What we will not add

A ghost who explains the war. A journal that fills in chapters as you walk. Ruins that exist only to drop a named sword. If a story shows up on Atoll, it shows up as a hole, a wall, or a name someone vouched.
