# Voices

The game does not talk over the player.

## Allowed

- One line in the world: the errand, the strike prompt, the thing you just identified.
- The skill line, the map fix, the friend tier.
- A paper, a charter, or a voucher that is an object. Someone wrote it. The sim did not.

## Refused

- A narrator.
- Bark trees. Companions do not quip.
- Quest text that tells you how you feel.
- A codex that unlocks lore entries for walking near a rock.

If two players need to know a fact, they tell each other, or the fact is in the crust. Appraisal is how you learn what a thing is. It is not how the plot advances.

Names of people are the names they carry. The planet's name is Cairn. The island's name is Atoll. Do not invent a king, a battle, or a year to fill a silence.
