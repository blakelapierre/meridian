# Voices

The game does not talk over the player. It does not keep a quest list.

## Allowed

- One line in the world: the strike prompt, the thing you just identified, a status word.
- The skill line, the map fix, the friend tier.
- A notebook of sentences the character was skilled enough to write. Past tense. No objectives.
- A sentence spoken to someone nearby, which they may fail to understand.
- A paper, a charter, or a voucher that is an object. Someone wrote it. The sim did not.

## Refused

- A quest UI, a tracker, a next step, a checkmark, a turn-in.
- A narrator.
- Bark trees. Companions do not quip.
- Quest text that tells you how you feel, or what to do next.
- A codex that unlocks lore entries for walking near a rock.

If two players need to know a fact, they tell each other, or the fact is in the crust. Appraisal is how you learn what a thing is. It is not how a plot advances.
