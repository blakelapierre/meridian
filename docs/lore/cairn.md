# Cairn

One planet. No shards, no reboot, no one behind the fight except the people in it.

## What everyone is told

The ground is older than the holds. People did not arrive as factions. They arrived as keys. What they did to the crust stayed. Night is not a coat of paint. The sun moves, flux dies, and a town that stored nothing goes dark in an order everyone can learn: lights, then fabbers, then anything that was pretending to be a wall.

Down is the core. A shot does not cross the curve. High ground is more of the planet, not a bonus tile.

## What is not canon

- A chosen one, a sealed evil, a final dungeon.
- Gods with spell schools. Practices are hands and devices. Resonance is a place in the crust under stress, and it is rare.
- Species select. The Litho is the ground answering, not a team.
- A war that ends.

## The three pressures

Holds stay. Hosts arrive with machines. The Veil sells the knowledge of which door matters. None of them own the planet. Allegiance is a pyramid of people, and it is allowed to rot when the patron stops providing land, rescue, or a hearth.

## Names

Cairn is the world. A cell is a few kilometers of it, not a server. A hearth is a stone you bind to, and breaking one is a cause, not a tooltip. A strike is a contract over a place, with a beginning and an end. Dominion is everything that is still there tomorrow.
