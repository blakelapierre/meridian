# Combat

From the concept. Physics is the combat language. Grains of sand are not simulated.

## In the build

The line is a hitscan against whatever body it meets. The dummy still takes the witnessed shot. Another player takes a blow on the harm line and can go down. A crab or a skink bites while you stand in reach. A goat or a gull can be dropped and does not bite. A dropped beast leaves a carcass you salvage with the hammer. It is not the dummy, and it does not pay the dummy's 12 metal. Townsfolk can be dropped. They get back up. They do not bite.

T plants a mission marker. A bolt in the air is pulled toward it. A pull also shifts a miss off the aim line. A planted crossbow looses a slower bolt of its own. It is not your line, and it pays 14 rather than a chest hit from the line. Another person standing in a fire marker's cell adds their name to it. There is no vehicle.

## This pass

A swing or a shot can meet a player or a beast. The wreck on the sand is a carcass, not the dummy.
