# Combat

From the concept. Physics is the combat language. Grains of sand are not simulated.

## In the build

The line is a hitscan against the dummy inside its range. Armor, drop, and penetration are not applied. The dummy clubs you if you stand on it. That is wilderness damage, not a firefight.

## Not in this build

- Small arms are instantaneous inside their range and real projectiles past it. Drag, drop, wind, and a penetration trace. Ricochet is angle and hardness, not a perk.
- Utility blocks vision and doors. It is not a sack of hit points.
- Melee matters underground. Rifles get worse in caves, breaches, and corridors.
- Vehicles fail by component: tracks, rotors, magazines, powerplants. A wreck is metal on the ground.
- Artillery is ballistic and radial. A ridge is cover until someone corrects a fire mission over it. Someone must see.
- A local fight is dozens of people, not hundreds. Past that, the overhead shows aggregates. The crater is still one place.

## Next when this opens

One projectile past the line's range, with drop, on the beach. A miss is a miss. No vehicle.
