# Life

Scrub, one tree, and four animals on the beach. The sand rises into hills you can walk.

## In the build

Hills rise to about three body heights, 5.4 m. Lows stay near the old beach. The feet stand on that height unless a pit is deeper. The camera stays above it. The picture marches the hill instead of flattening it. Five scrubs, a dry tuft, and a short tree sit on the ground. A crab and a skink wander and step away when you come close. A gull circles. They are not targets, and they are not written into the chain.

The animals use the wall clock, so a slow extra view does not make them crawl. Coming close pushes the crab and the skink away. The gull does not land. A jump is measured from the hill under you, not from the old flat beach. The hill is drawn across the whole island. The sea has no far clip. Nothing in this build is fog. A grazed patch loses one stock, marks a track, and is dead at zero. That patch is also a static graze animal in the world picture. It does not walk.
