# People

## In the build

Other clients are bodies. Tab cycles them, and it also cycles Pell, Hask, and Nim, who stand in Southmere and do not walk. U speaks to one of them if you are within 3 m. The target plate names whatever you have selected. Esc, Backspace, or Clear drops it. Standing inside sense range raises friendliness by 1 a second, up to 100. What you trade depends on that score and on mapping, mining, building, and appraisal on both sides. The AI clients are the same view, driven only through player keys.

While you drag someone, they are drawn just ahead of you. Their client is sent one drag step on the control channel and takes it. They cannot refuse.
