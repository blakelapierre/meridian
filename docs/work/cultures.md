# Cultures

From the concept. Creation is a body and a key, not a role. Three starting cultures are equipment and neighbors. You can leave them.

## In the build

Everyone wakes in a culture. The default is Holds. `?culture=hosts` starts with more metal and less stone. `?culture=veil` starts with less of both. Skills do not change. You can still walk away with what you are carrying.

## This pass

A specialty is marked with a star on the skill list. Two are still the cap.
