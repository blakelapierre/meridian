# Cultures

From the concept. Creation is a body and a key, not a role. Three starting cultures are equipment and neighbors. You can leave them.

## In the build

Everyone wakes in a culture. The default is Holds. `?culture=hosts` starts with more metal and less stone. `?culture=veil` starts with less of both. Skills do not change. You can still walk away with what you are carrying.

A trainer stands by the table. Using them can mark a specialty, and a third is refused. The trainer raises the first empty skill to 8 when nothing is high enough to specialize. It will not raise a skill that is already at 40.

## This pass

Walking into the north cell puts down the kit you woke with. Metal and stone earned after that stay.
