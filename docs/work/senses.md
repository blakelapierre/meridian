# Senses

## In the build

The map is a picture that stays. Looking paints sand, pits, stone, and wood. The wheel over the chart zooms it. Dragging pans it. Skills widen the survey, the names, and the places. Appraisal names a target. Eyesight and rangefinding open the first-person zoom and the range line.

## This pass

The chart readout says `sense` and the radius in meters. The dashed ring is that radius. Told ground stays cooler.
