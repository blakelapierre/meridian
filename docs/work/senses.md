# Senses

## In the build

The map is a picture that stays. Looking paints the color of that ground: sand, grass, rock, pits, stone, and wood. Lows are darker and crests are lighter. Topography, under mapping, draws the contours and refines them. M cycles a small map, a full-screen map, and no map. The small map opens first. The wheel over the chart zooms it. Dragging pans it. Skills widen the survey, the names, and the places. Appraisal names a target. Eyesight and rangefinding open the first-person zoom and the range line.

## This pass

Subskills sit under the skill they come from. They rise only from that practice, and they cannot pass the parent. Prospect records a pit. Bracing strengthens a new wall. Focus extends the zoom. Measure refines the range. Trailing draws other bodies on the map. Landing softens a fall. Wind spends less stamina on a sprint.
