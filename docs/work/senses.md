Farther out the mark opens into a ring. Past sense range it is not on the chart. Appraisal names a target. Eyesight and rangefinding open the first-person zoom and the range line. The chart draws a solid sight edge and its line reads `sight N m`. The ground on the chart is what nearby players trade. Grass, rock, sand, and pits go across, not a flat sand note.
