# Senses

## In the build

The map is a picture that stays. Looking paints the color of that ground: sand, grass, rock, pits, stone, and wood. Lows are darker and crests are lighter. M cycles a small map, a full-screen map, and no map. The small map opens first. The wheel over the chart zooms it. Dragging pans it. Skills widen the survey, the names, and the places. Appraisal names a target. Eyesight and rangefinding open the first-person zoom and the range line.

## This pass

Topography is a subskill of mapping. It is listed under mapping. It rises only while the chart is surveying, and it cannot pass mapping. No contour lines until it reaches 4. Then the interval falls from 4 m to 2 m, 1 m, and 0.5 m, and the hill is sampled on a finer grid.
