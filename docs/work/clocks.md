# Clocks

From the concept. One body, three clocks. You do not pick a mode. You zoom.

## In the build

Hands are the default. A strike is a contract you walk into. Leaving the volume for five seconds puts the beach body back. When a live round becomes the gap and you did not walk out, the yard gains 8 metal. Walking out pays nothing.

Sitting at the table names the clock `dominion`. Standing returns it to `hands`. It is not a second map. Sitting returns an overhead camera pose for the place you are: pitch −1.2, height 18. The picture camera stays on the body.
