# Clocks

From the concept. One body, three clocks. You do not pick a mode. You zoom.

## In the build

Hands are the default: look, move, dig, place, shoot. A strike is a contract you walk into. Leaving the volume for five seconds puts the beach body back. There is no dominion layer.

## Not in this build

| Clock | Scale | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| Hands | Tenths of a second | Shoot, dig, drive, breach, carry |
| Strike | Minutes | Buy, execute, hold a site. Only the fight resets |
| Dominion | Hours to months | Deeds, allegiance, factories, roads, the dayline |

A session that only shoots is valid. A session that only hauls is valid. Power accumulates in the layer you neglect. There is no global win.

## Next when this opens

The strike pays the company in ground or materials when the round ends, and pays nothing for walking out. Dominion stays closed until a deed can exist without a menu.

## Later

The overhead is a zoom of the same place, not a second game. Command and resonance stay locked until the hands make sense.
