# Deeds

From the concept. Deeds reserve volume. They are crude law the sim enforces. They do not enforce speech, manners, or peace.

## In the build

A bound hearth claims a 6 m radius. The binder may edit it. A stranger's certified dig inside it is refused. Upkeep is not charged.

## This pass

H, while you have a hearth and a person targeted, hands them a key. A key may edit the claim. The holder still can.
