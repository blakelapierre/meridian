## In the build

The crate starts as 30 metal, 8 stone, and one solar fin. The footing anchors pieces. A wall costs 6 metal and must touch that support within eight steps. Building skill shortens the raise and raises the wall's integrity. Salvage returns the 6 metal. The hearth costs 20 metal and 8 stone. The fin costs 8 metal. Both replicate to peers. Southmere, west of the crate, is a public hearth and roofs in the atoll props. Binding there does not spend the hearth recipe.

F places a floor for 2 stone. A post may stand on that floor, and the floor counts as support. A beam can hold a floor. That floor is marked held. A door on a beam closes that span. H, with the hammer, plants a crossbow for 8 metal on supported ground. It watches 16 m and looses on its own at a beast, the dummy, or another player. Salvage returns 4.
