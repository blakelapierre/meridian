# Yard

## In the build

The crate starts as 30 metal, 8 stone, and one solar fin. The footing anchors pieces. A wall costs 6 metal and must touch that support within eight steps. Building skill shortens the raise and raises the wall's integrity. Salvage returns the 6 metal. The hearth costs 20 metal and 8 stone. The fin costs 8 metal. Both replicate to peers.

## This pass

A wall that loses its path to the footing falls. Its metal does not return to the wallet. Half, rounded down, becomes spoil on the yard. One fallen wall is 3 spoil. The line shows `spoil` once any has fallen.

## Proves

`test/build.test.js` still expects the salvaged metal. The fallen wall's spoil is asserted there too.
