# Command

From the concept. The chair is a place, and it is vulnerable.

## In the build

There is no war table, no overhead, and no company wallet. The AI clients are people, not orders.

## Not in this build

Any specialized commander at a live war table — a room, a crawler bridge, a field kit — may take the overhead of the company's sensed world. The table is furniture. Destroy it and the commander is a person again, standing where the table was.

From the chair you spend team logistics: queues, extractions, radar, fire missions, supply drops, fabber routes. You ping and order. You do not pilot anyone. The company wallet cannot dress a soldier. Kit is what they carried or earned.

Fog is physical. The overview is not omniscience. A client is not sent a person no sensor, eye, or elevation has earned.

A company can fight without a commander. It is slower and blinder. A commander without soldiers is a person shouting at fabbers.

## Next when this opens

Nothing, until a company can exist. The keymap already reserves the table: while seated, W A S D pan and the wheel zooms. That camera is not a vehicle.

## Later

The seat, the spend, and the rule that breaking the table drops you on your feet.
