# Litho

From the concept. The crust's immune system. Not a faction you can select.

## In the build

Nothing on the beach answers when you dig. A pit stays a pit. Off the beach, a piece with nobody within 24 m loses one integrity a tick. That rule is not applied on Atoll.

Off the beach, once the resonance word is showing, one swarm sphere stands near you. South of y = −190 is off the beach. The beach has none. The swarm flees when you are near and bites when you are close. A bite adds one pain.

## This pass
