# Litho

From the concept. The crust's immune system. Not a faction you can select.

## In the build

Nothing answers when you dig. A pit stays a pit.

## Not in this build

Spore timber eats unattended metal. Burrow swarms show up in over-mined cells. Storms follow heavy resonance. Strip-mine a hemisphere and the hemisphere answers.

Too little and the world is a quarry. Too much and people fight weather instead of each other. The amount is still an open question.

## Next when this opens

Not on the beach. The first sign, when it comes, is a placed piece with no one nearby that loses integrity. No creature, no storm.

## Later

Swarms, spore timber, storms tied to resonance. Resonance itself stays rare and locational.
