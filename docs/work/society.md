# Society

From the concept. Three social objects, on purpose not merged.

## In the build

Friendliness is a score with someone you stand near. It gates how much of the map you trade. It is not a fellowship, not a township, and not an allegiance.

## Not in this build

| Object | What it is |
|---|---|
| Fellowship | The people with you tonight. Temporary. Shared sight. They may drag and loot you immediately |
| Township | A deed, a charter, a maintenance bill. Geographic |
| Allegiance | Vassal, patron, monarch. Political. Not the same as friendship |

Pass-up is surplus mastery, scaled by loyalty and leadership, plus a tithe you agreed to. It is not a hidden cut of the vassal's own skill. A patron who does not provide land, rescue, or a hearth full of people gets abandoned. The pyramid may rot.

Names can be vouched by an allegiance. A new key can always be minted. Reputation does not follow it.

## Next when this opens

A fellowship of the people in sense range who have both accepted. Shared sight means their map picture is offered at friend tier while the fellowship lasts. It ends when someone leaves or the session ends. No patron.

## Later

Township charters. Vassal, patron, monarch. Tithe. Vouched names.
