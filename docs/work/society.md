# Society

From the concept. Three social objects, on purpose not merged.

## In the build

Friendliness is still a score. Y offers a fellowship to the person you have targeted. If they have stood near you long enough to be an acquaintance, they accept. While it lasts, map trade uses friend tier. It ends when they leave range, or when you shoot them.

The first fellowship is a patron. A vein tap pays them 1 metal, counted on you, not handed over. The tithe is taken from you and added to the patron's purse. That purse can pay one missed upkeep. It cannot buy a wall. A wall still costs yard metal.
